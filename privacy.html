Privacy Policy

Short version: we use your details only to answer your enquiry, and one advertising cookie only if you accept it.

Who we are

This website advertises the private sale of a property in Couture-d'Argenson, France, by its owners. Prospective buyers can contact the owners through the enquiry form on this website.

What we collect

When you submit the enquiry form we receive the details you enter: name, email, phone, preferred contact time, purchase timescale, financing preference and your message, plus the campaign parameters (UTM tags) that show which advertisement brought you here.

How we use it

Solely to respond to your enquiry about the property and to arrange viewings or share further details you request. We do not sell, rent or share your details with third parties for marketing. The lawful basis is your consent and our legitimate interest in responding to an enquiry you initiated.

Cookies and advertising measurement

If — and only if — you click "Accept" on the cookie notice, this site loads the Meta (Facebook) Pixel, which sets cookies used to measure whether our advertising works and to report anonymous conversion statistics to Meta Platforms Ireland Ltd. If you decline, no advertising cookies are set. You can change your mind by clearing this site's data in your browser. See Meta's own policy at facebook.com/privacy for how Meta processes this data.

Retention

Enquiry details are kept for the duration of the sale process and deleted within 12 months of the property's sale completing, unless we are corresponding with you about a purchase.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time, and you may withdraw consent for cookies. Contact us at the address above. You may also lodge a complaint with your supervisory authority (in France, the CNIL: cnil.fr).
