The Story

A Village Life, Within Reach

Some houses ask you to imagine a different life. This one has already built it.

At the quiet heart of Couture-d'Argenson — a working village where the bakery, the bar and the school are all a short stroll from the gate — four buildings gather around a sunlit courtyard. Lavender spills down the front bank. The bread is warm by eight.

This is four opportunities in one: a renovated family home, two established holiday gîtes that have earned their keep for years, and a large outbuilding waiting for whatever you dream up next — studio, atelier, or a third gîte in time.

The Property

Four Buildings, One Address

Chapter I

The Main House

At the heart of the property, this renovated split-level French village house combines generous living space with an easy connection to the gardens. A spacious country kitchen is complemented by a long living and dining room opening onto a private terrace and the lavender-framed front garden.

Three comfortable bedrooms and a small home office provide flexible everyday space, with one bedroom and a cosy sitting area tucked beneath the characterful attic beams. On the lower level, a laundry room and discreet lockable connection to the Garden Gîte create excellent potential for multigenerational living or income-producing holiday accommodation.
