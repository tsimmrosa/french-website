The Story

A Village Life, Within Reach

Some houses ask you to imagine a different life. This one has already built it.

At the quiet heart of Couture-d'Argenson — a working village where the bakery, the bar and the school are all a short stroll from the gate — four buildings gather around a sunlit courtyard. Lavender spills down the front bank. The bread is warm by eight.

This is four opportunities in one: a renovated family home, two established holiday gîtes that have earned their keep for years, and a large outbuilding waiting for whatever you dream up next — studio, atelier, or a third gîte in time.

Grounds & Lifestyle

Where the Garden Meets the Pool

Mornings begin in the vegetable garden; afternoons end at the covered outdoor bar — the Place de l'Apéro, as the sign insists — with the plancha warming and the pool holding the last of the light.

Around it: 3,200 m² of lawns, silver birches, a shaded gazebo, a young orchard, and a central courtyard that ties the four buildings together. Off-street parking, mains everything, no surprises.

And beyond the white gate, the village does the rest — bakery, bar-restaurant, pharmacy, school and doctor, all within a stroll.
